    Architecture

    Launchpad: from token to royalty payout

    value or data flowreturn, async or externalcore system
    1. LaunchThe artist sets a raise goal and the share of album profits that goes to holders, then signs the agreement.
    2. RaiseFans buy tokens in USDC before release. The raise closes at the goal, and the artist receives it minus a 5% launch fee (placeholder).
    3. ReleaseThe album ships through the artist’s distributor as normal. Masters stay with the artist.
    4. CollectRoyalty statements go to the trustee, who verifies them and funds the payout vault.
    5. Pay outEach period, the vault splits the holders’ share pro rata across every token, in USDC.

    Prediction: how a market resolves

    value or data flowreturn, async or externalcore system
    1. ListThe artist sets a verifiable milestone, a resolution date and seed liquidity in the registry.
    2. DeployThe registry creates a binary market. Yes and No prices always sum to $1.
    3. TradeFans buy shares on the order book. 1% of volume streams to the artist treasury (placeholder fee).
    4. ResolveAt expiry an oracle proposer posts the outcome from the named data source, and a dispute window follows.
    5. RedeemWinning shares pay $1.00 USDC each. The artist keeps every fee, win or lose.
