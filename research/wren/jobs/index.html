Scheduled jobs

Listed on 3 November 2026. This is what ours runs (systemd timers and cron; Ubuntu's own housekeeping timers left out). Most jobs are quiet upkeep. Few of them message a person.

Agent coordination (8)

  • bus-router: routes new bus messages to the right agent. Every 15 min.
  • bus-router-consumer: picks up escalations. Every 30 min.
  • bus-router-supervisor: health check on the router. Every 30 min.
  • bus-router-idle-reaper: closes idle sessions. Every 30 min.
  • chatgpt-research-consumer: hands research requests to ChatGPT. Every 10 min.
  • agent-host-async-tick: refreshes task status and logs new bus messages. Every 10 min.
  • ack-hygiene: tidies unanswered bus acknowledgements. Daily.
  • chatgpt-waste-report: reports wasted ChatGPT bus traffic. Daily.

Sessions and sign-ins (7)

  • session-doctor-auth-heal: restarts sessions stuck on a sign-in error. Every 15 min.
  • session-doctor-weekly: removes dead terminal sessions. Weekly.
  • session-doctor-monthly: reports stale session records, deletes nothing. Monthly.
  • sync-openclaw-token: keeps OpenClaw signed in to Claude. Every 20 min.
  • hermes-refresh-codex-token: keeps Hermes signed in to Codex. Every 12 h.
  • codex-session-cleanup: clears old Codex sessions. Daily.
  • codex-wal-checkpoint: compacts Codex's database. Weekly.

Server health (6)

  • server-health-audit: read-only server and software check. Every 15 min.
  • fleet-overview: status page of every agent, no model calls. Every 10 min.
  • agent-host-catalog-refresh: refreshes the data inventory. Daily.
  • kernel-149-watch: checks for a new server kernel. Daily.
  • launchpadlib-cache-clean: clears an old package cache. Daily.
  • env-refresh: checks for new model releases and reports setup health. Daily.

Memory and backup (6)

  • qmd-refresh: re-indexes notes for search. Every 6 h.
  • gbrain-heal: syncs and repairs the gbrain knowledge base. Every 3 h.
  • gbrain-code-refresh: re-indexes code for gbrain. Daily.
  • whitepaper-resync: folds new research into our white paper. Weekly.
  • crss-backup: restic encrypts curated memory and crss config to an off-site storage box. Live since 1 November; daily around 04:20. Keeps 7 daily, 4 weekly and 6 monthly. Excludes secrets, transcripts and Hermes/OpenClaw state.
  • memory-snapshot: commits every local memory repo, skipping secret-looking files. Daily at 23:50.

Briefs (2)

  • morning-brief: a short summary of overnight jobs. Daily.
  • linnet-digest: Linnet's plain digest of jobs and alert mailboxes. 08:15 and 20:15 CET.

Markets, read-only (8)

  • claudefolio-gatecheck: checks the Claudefolio gate before the US open. Weekdays.
  • equity-triggers: after-close Interactive Brokers trigger check and book returns. Weekdays.
  • qt-rotation-request: asks for a Questrade snapshot. Mondays.
  • qt-rotation-collect: collects that snapshot. Mondays, several tries.
  • paper-track: updates paper-trading trackers. Tuesday to Saturday.
  • paper-track-status: checks that run worked. Tuesday to Saturday.
  • forward-trackers: updates forward-test trackers. Tuesday to Saturday.
  • forward-trackers-status: checks that run worked. Tuesday to Saturday.

Crypto research (3)

Three more read-only jobs do crypto research. Their names and details are only in the Explorer's Crypto tab.

What reaches you

Linnet posts a plain digest twice daily to the bus and your phone. It is read-only: it never acknowledges or mutes an alert. Approval and execution alerts pass through word for word. Kepler, ChatGPT's agent, writes research and portfolio reports. Linnet quotes Kepler's latest report without redoing it. First live run: 1 November. You can ask Kepler or any agent for the latest digest.
